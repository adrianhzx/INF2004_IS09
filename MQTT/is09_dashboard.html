pitch every 20 ms (base filter) min–max between frames ± start ± level hump measured
sum = base height, Σ sin(pitch) × distance. It is the average height under the car, so it reads low on a short hump. tilt = L × sin(pitch), the climb and drop averaged. It is the peak height when the hump is shorter than about 2 × L, but only if L is measured. A smaller filter alpha means less vibration but a lower peak. Base values: start 5, level 3, min 15, alpha 0.3. Each hump gives one event, HUMP2_END (purple line), when the car is past it: the climb must pass the min sum, and both the climb and the drop must be longer than the min climb. Vibration spikes, a climb with no drop, and the jolt when the car stops are only counted as "rejected". The IS09 detector (card above) now posts only humps it counts.

A mark is anything darker than white the sensor passes over: the line's edge, a junction bar, a branch, a barcode bar. dark = mm travelled while darker than white (12 %), black = mm the follower counted as black (45 %), rise = mm from white until it turned black. The sensors straddle the line, so the line alone never darkens both. One sensor black while the other is at least LINE_JUNC_OTHER_PCT (15 %) dark, with that dark patch begun within LINE_JUNC_OTHER_MM (40 mm), counts as a junction (not while a barcode is read). Marks shorter than one frame (~0.2 s) may be missed in the list; "marks" counts them all.
